Neon Focus Guard — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 September 2026. Last updated: 30 September 2026, review build 1.28 / code 29.

Developer contact: neonfocuscore@gmail.com.

Repository: Neon Focus.

Local data and app lock

Profile settings, usage totals, selected app limits, alarms, timer/stopwatch state and Goal Timing names/dates remain in app-private storage. General preferences are not all encrypted. The optional app PIN is never stored as plaintext: a randomly salted PBKDF2 verifier is encrypted with an Android Keystore AES-GCM key. Hardware backing depends on the device. Biometric templates stay with Android. Authentication remains in process memory and is lost when the process ends; locking also follows the selected background delay, screen-off or Lock now.

Photos and widgets

Only a photo you select through Android's picker is copied into private storage and resized to at most 768 pixels for clock, timer, stopwatch or goal countdown backgrounds. No gallery-wide permission or photo upload is used. Remove photo deletes its private copy. Widgets may show your photo, goal name and countdown to anyone able to view the home screen. Goal Timing has no floating orb. The app keeps the screen awake while its activities are visible; it does not change the device-wide screen timeout.

Permissions

  • Usage Access: calculates app time and enforces chosen limits locally. Usage history is not uploaded.
  • Accessibility: detects foreground app/window identity, shows reminders and lock overlays, and supports PiP/Recent Apps controls. It may read launcher task-card app labels to close a locked app. It does not read messages, passwords, browser history or webpage text. Optional pixel classification is a separate MediaProjection feature.
  • Notification Access: controls supported playback and dismisses clearable notifications for restricted apps. Optional Notification shield dismisses ordinary third-party notifications while preserving critical/system categories and ongoing services. Notification text and sender details are not saved or uploaded.
  • Do Not Disturb access: lets Notification shield activate its own Android rule. Calls are silenced, not rejected. Disabling the shield releases its rule and preserves other DND rules.
  • Device Admin: adds Android's administrator deactivation step before uninstalling. It cannot guarantee prevention of force-stop or uninstall. No remote wipe or password-reset capability is used.
  • Display over other apps: shows protection reminders outside Neon Focus. No goal orb is included.
  • Microphone: Friend listens after a tap. The app requests compatible Android on-device Hindi recognition and does not retain recordings. A system speech-model download may need internet. Voice replies and songs are bundled offline.
  • Notifications, exact alarms and full-screen alarms: provide visible media/service controls and scheduled ringing, subject to Android permissions and device restrictions.

Optional Shorts and social app control

More contains one opt-in control, off by default. When enabled, YouTube Shorts viewers are identified using exact visible resource IDs, layout geometry and player structure. Processing happens locally; this feature does not read or save video titles, searches, messages, accessibility text, screenshots or recordings. A confirmed Shorts viewer returns to Home with a silent 10-second visual effect. Ordinary YouTube screens remain available unless another independently configured protection applies. Unknown YouTube layouts are allowed.

The same control blocks the complete official listed social, messaging and entertainment Android apps by package identity, returning Home with the existing 18-second visual effect and offline voice. It does not classify their feeds. Disabling through Neon Focus uses the existing two-voice/read/1-to-20 confirmation. Completing it removes this control immediately; cancelling preserves it. Separate daily limits, today's blocks and Screen safety remain independent. Android permissions remain revocable in Settings.

An exact, locally reviewed study-app catalog excludes supported learning, notes, PDF and math tools from the 30-minute reminder only. It neither reads Play Store activity on the phone nor uploads the installed-app list. Daily limits and explicit locks still apply. Optional Block for today stores a selected app's deadline until the next local midnight; it has a confirmation before activation and cannot be disabled inside Neon Focus that day. Android system controls and clock changes can affect enforcement.

Visual screen safety

With your explicit Android screen-sharing permission, capturable frames are processed in memory by local LiteRT/ONNX models, then discarded. Frames are not recorded or uploaded. Secure, blank and excluded screens cannot be classified. Detection can miss unsafe content or mistakenly flag safe content. Android's capture indicator and stop control remain available. Restarting/updating the app or ending capture may require fresh consent. Optional model downloads use HTTPS and SHA-256 verification; the download host sees ordinary network request information such as IP address.

DNS guard and internet traffic

A local DNS-only VPN checks domain rules and uses Cloudflare Family DNS (1.1.1.3 / 1.0.0.3) for upstream lookups. Domain lookups therefore reach the DNS provider; the app does not send browsing content or usage history. Normal app traffic stays on the physical network, is not proxied through a remote VPN, and HTTPS is not decrypted. Other VPNs, encrypted DNS, cached addresses and direct IPs can bypass this layer. Android's Block connections without VPN must be off for this DNS-only design.

Purchases and external services

Lifetime billing is disabled in this review build. When enabled after external setup, Google Play/Play Integrity and an HTTPS entitlement service verify purchases and trial eligibility using purchase tokens, integrity tokens and a random installation identifier. Screen frames, app PINs and usage history are not sent for billing. Verified receipts are cached with signed responses and Android Keystore encryption. Google Device Recall may remember trial/lifetime status after reinstall; deployment retention and server-side deletion procedures must be finalized before billing is released. No advertising SDK or cloud analytics is included.

One Life and Brahmacharya currently display only an upcoming message. They do not activate another app, new tracking or account creation.

Your controls and deletion

Permissions can be revoked in Android Settings. Removing a selected photo deletes its private copy; clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local settings and data. Device Admin must first be deactivated to uninstall. Local safety-event history can be cleared from its Guard dialog. Uninstalling does not erase Google Play purchase records or future server/Device Recall records; contact the developer for relevant server-side requests. Generated local developer test screenshots are not an app feature and are excluded from Git.

Contact

For questions or data requests, email neonfocuscore@gmail.com.

Local previews and Friend introduction

Account and More Features are upcoming previews only: no account creation, community uploads or app hiding. The Friend introduction uses bundled video and voice. Its animated waves use a precomputed voice amplitude envelope, not microphone recording. Playback pauses when leaving the page. Icons for installed social and entertainment apps are loaded locally and are not uploaded.
